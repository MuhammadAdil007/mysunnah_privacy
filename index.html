Privacy Policy

My Sunnah — by DevBatch

Effective Date: October 29, 2026  |  Last Updated: October 29, 2026

DevBatch ("we," "us," or "our") operates the My Sunnah mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our App.

By using My Sunnah, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide Directly

  • Account Information: When you sign up, we collect your name, email address, and password (encrypted via Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile Information: Optional profile picture, gender, age, location (city/country), and preferred language.
  • User Content: Prayer tracking data, fitness goals, dua favorites, Quran bookmarks, and reflection notes you create inside the app.
  • Communications: Messages you send us for support via email.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, mobile network information, and language settings.
  • Usage Data: Features used, screens visited, time spent in the app, and interaction patterns (via Firebase Analytics).
  • Crash & Performance Data: Error logs and crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics) to improve app stability.
  • Location Data: With your permission, we access your device's approximate or precise location only to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction. Location data is processed on-device and is not stored on our servers.

1.3 Information from Third Parties

  • In-App Purchases: When you purchase a premium subscription, Google Play Billing (Android) or Apple App Store (iOS) processes the transaction. We receive confirmation of purchase and subscription status but do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Live Events (Agora): If you join a live stream or event, Agora processes audio/video streams. See Agora's Privacy Policy.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide and maintain the App's core features (prayer times, Quran, hadith, duas, fitness, halal scanner).
  • Deliver personalized content (e.g., prayer notifications based on your location).
  • Process premium subscription purchases and manage your subscription.
  • Send push notifications (azan, prayer reminders, event alerts) — you can disable these in your device settings.
  • Improve the App through analytics and crash reporting.
  • Respond to customer support inquiries.
  • Enforce our Terms of Service and prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Retention

  • Account data is retained as long as your account is active.
  • Usage and analytics data is retained for up to 26 months.
  • Crash reports are retained for up to 90 days.
  • You can request deletion of your account and all associated data by emailing subscriptions@devbatch.com. We will respond within 30 days.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location (GDPR for EU, CCPA for California, etc.), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your account and personal data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Restrict or object to processing of your data.
  • Data portability — receive a copy of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g., disable location, revoke notifications in device settings).

To exercise any of these rights, email subscriptions@devbatch.com.

6. Children's Privacy

My Sunnah is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information:

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Passwords are hashed and stored securely via Firebase Authentication.
  • Firestore data is protected by security rules that restrict access to authenticated users only.
  • Payment processing is handled entirely by Google Play / Apple — we never store your card details.

However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. International Data Transfers

Your data may be processed in countries outside your own, including the United States (where Firebase and Agora servers are located). By using the App, you consent to such transfers. We ensure appropriate safeguards (e.g., Standard Contractual Clauses) are in place.

9. Permissions We Request

The App may request the following device permissions:

  • Location — for accurate prayer times and Qibla direction.
  • Notifications — for azan and prayer reminders.
  • Camera — for the halal product scanner (barcode scanning).
  • Microphone & Camera — for participating in live events (only when you join).
  • Storage — for caching Quran audio and offline content.
  • Internet — for syncing data and streaming content.

You can revoke any of these permissions at any time via your device settings.

10. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services (e.g., Islamic scholars, charity organizations). We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their policies independently.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified through an in-app prompt or email. The "Last Updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
